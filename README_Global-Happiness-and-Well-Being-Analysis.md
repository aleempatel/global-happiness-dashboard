# 🌍 Global Happiness and Well-Being Analysis

A Power BI dashboard project analyzing the **World Happiness Report** (2015–2019) to understand what factors drive happiness across countries and regions. Built as part of Week 2 of the Live Pakistan Internship Program.

## 📁 Project Overview

This project examines global happiness data across five years to explore how factors like GDP, social support, health, freedom, trust in government, and generosity relate to a country's overall happiness score.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Rank and compare the happiest and least happy countries globally
- Analyze regional differences in happiness scores
- Study correlations between happiness and key factors (GDP, social support, health, freedom, trust, generosity)
- Track how happiness factors changed from 2015 to 2019
- Present findings through an interactive Power BI dashboard

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI** – Data modeling, DAX measures, and interactive dashboard design
- **CSV** – World Happiness Report datasets (2015–2019)

## 🔍 Key Insights

- GDP per capita and social support show strong positive correlation with happiness
- Western Europe and North America consistently rank among the happiest regions
- Freedom and trust in government have a notable but smaller impact on happiness
- Happiness rankings remained relatively stable across 2015–2019 for top-ranked countries

## 🚀 How to View

1. Download and open `Global Happiness Dashboard.pbix` in **Power BI Desktop**.
2. Explore the interactive visuals, filters, and slicers to drill down into the data by year, region, and country.
